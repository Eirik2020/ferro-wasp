# ADR-0062: Faults Have Classes, Hard Faults Latch, And The Failsafe Follows Betaflight

Status: accepted, not yet implemented; changes the RC-loss response (W6)

Decided by the user on 2026-10-20.

**Two paths.** A consuming task's gate reports hard and failsafe-level faults
straight to the safety master. Data below that goes to the Health Manager
(ADR-0061), which classifies it and may combine it into a hard fault. The
safety master acts on the class.

| Class | While armed | While disarmed |
|---|---|---|
| hard fault | disarm at once (W2) | refuse arming |
| failsafe-level | the failsafe below | refuse arming |
| land immediately | no automatic action; an urgent warning | refuse arming while present |
| fault | no flight effect; reported | refuse arming until it clears |
| fault-imminent | reported | reported; arming allowed |

**Latching.** A hard fault latches, and only a reboot releases it: after a
hard fault the board is not trusted until it restarts and passes its boot
checks. Failsafe-level and lower faults do not latch; they clear when their
condition clears.

**Land immediately** means the user must get the system into a safe state as
quickly as possible, like a low-battery warning: the drone will crash unless
they land immediately. It is raised by a failed border role (ADR-0061).

**RC loss is failsafe-level**, not a hard fault, reported straight from
Command Input's gate. It does not latch: once the link is valid again, arming
is allowed after the normal checks.

**The failsafe mirrors Betaflight**: a stage 1 guard period, then the stage 2
procedure. Until angle mode is flight-verified, stage 2 can only be a drop
(disarm). Betaflight's stage timings, stage 1 channel values and recovery
rules are taken from its source at implementation. This replaces today's
disarm on the first invalid frame, a W6 change that needs props-off
fault-injection evidence.

Today every fault ends in a disarm: RC loss revokes the arm permit, disarms
and sends `ActuatorCmd::Disarm`, with no gentler failsafe, because no angle
mode exists to descend with.

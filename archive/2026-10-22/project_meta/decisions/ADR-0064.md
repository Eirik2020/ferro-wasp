# ADR-0064: Actuator Output Confirms Arming And Cross-Checks Disarming

Status: accepted, not yet implemented

Decided by the user on 2026-10-20.

**Arming is confirmed before it is declared.** Command Input requests arming;
the safety master checks and, if safe, asks Actuator Output to arm; Actuator
Output runs its arming sequence and reports success or failure; only on
success does the safety master declare the system armed. Actuator Output
judges its own success, fresh in-range eRPM on every motor (ADR-0040), from a
link of its own from actuator feedback. The handshake already exists as
`SafetyEvent::ArmRequested` and `SafetyEvent::ActuatorIdling`.

**Disarming is immediate and unconditional**: every task disarms at once,
with no round trip.

**The arm-state cross-check.** Actuator Output is the only task that writes
the actuators. It holds its own actuator-armed state and compares it with the
system-armed flag:

| | actuator armed | actuator disarmed |
|---|---|---|
| system armed | efforts accepted | efforts refused; fault to the safety master |
| system disarmed | Actuator Output disarms the actuators | efforts refused |

The gates on Command Input, the control loop and Actuator Output overlap, so
a failure one misses another catches.

**Disarm until it succeeds.** DShot has no acknowledgement: the firmware
knows what it sent, not what an ESC did.

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| accepted | Actuator Output drops its armed state at once and refuses every throttle command |
| succeeded | a stop frame set completed without a fault, or the bank faulted and forced the lines low; either way Actuator Output reports "actuators disarmed"; a faulted bank stays down and is not retried |
| re-asserted | stop frames continue every period while disarmed (ADR-0046's fallback keeps frames at no less than 500 Hz): the retry loop |
| corroborated | where bidirectional eRPM exists, eRPM falls below a threshold within a spin-down limit; a motor still spinning past it is a hard fault, an ESC ignoring stop, and stop keeps being sent |

Spin-down is a cross-check, not the success condition: it depends on the prop
and its inertia. A low line is a signal loss, on which the ESC stops its
motor; BLHeli_32's signal-loss timeout is checked at implementation.

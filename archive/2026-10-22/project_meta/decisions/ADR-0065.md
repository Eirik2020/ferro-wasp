# ADR-0065: Hardware Transports Live In The Backend, Application Glue In One Crate

Status: accepted, not yet implemented; amends ADR-0060

Decided by the user on 2026-10-21.

ADR-0060 put each task in its subsystem's crate behind a `tasks` feature.
Moving them showed three constraints ADR-0060 did not weigh, and the user
settled each:

**Tasks name hardware only through neutral traits.** A task names the
hardware-neutral traits and types of `ferrowasp-stm32-critical` (or, for a
non-critical task, `ferrowasp-stm32`) through FerroForge `bounds`, as most
already did; the firmware binds the chip family's types. The two chip-family
uses that remained become neutral: the IMU's data-ready pin is a trait, and
the USB wake a firmware-supplied function. No subsystem crate takes a chip
feature. Giving each crate the family backend was the alternative.

**The hardware transports live in the backend.** `ferrowasp-stm32-critical`
depends on `ferrowasp-imu`, `-output` and `-serial`, so their tasks cannot
live there without a cycle. The SPI1 IMU group, the DShot service, fallback
and DMA completions, and the UART transports are the backend's hardware
mechanisms, and ADR-0057 already puts the boundary between hardware and core
at message contracts: they move behind a `tasks` feature in
`ferrowasp-stm32-critical`, and `imu`, `output` and `serial` keep the logic
and the traits. Inverting the traits into those crates was the alternative,
a refactor that overlaps ADR-0057.

**Four tasks wait for the message contracts.** Actuator output drives the
DShot bank and reads the motor map; the DShot service reports to the safety
master and to the ESC manager; the ESC manager and RC input hold the
backend's serial port endpoint, and RC input applies flight-control's rates.
Wherever they go now, two crates depend on each other. They move with
ADR-0057's `MotorCommand`, `ActuatorFeedback` and `RxChunk` links, which
remove the coupling.

**Application glue is one crate.** The facade macro, the task groups, the
channel constructors and the reports the command lines build from the ledger
stay together in `ferrowasp-stm32-app`, `ferrowasp-stm32-tasks` renamed and
emptied of task bodies, non-critical; one copy serves every board. A copy in
each firmware was the alternative.

**Homes the map did not give.** The flash manager, the USB task, the
configurator link and the heartbeat go to `ferrowasp-cli`: the flash manager
cannot sit in `ferrowasp-storage`, below the blackbox and command-line crates
it uses. RC telemetry joins the OSD in `ferrowasp-osd`; the ADC tasks and
the status LEDs go to `ferrowasp-stm32`. The values the coordinator will take
in (the host-connected flags, the accelerometer level) wait in
`ferrowasp-border::inbound`, beside it, so the control loop reads them from a
safety-critical crate.

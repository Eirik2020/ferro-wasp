Shared fixtures for the onboard blackbox format.

foxeer-f405-2khz.fwbb
    The first 400 pages (102400 bytes) of flight 13 from the Foxeer F405 V2,
    as downloaded on 2026-10-15 into
    logs/foxeer-flights/20261015-before-dfu-reboot/flight-13.fwbb. Recorded
    by older firmware at a 2000 Hz control loop with log divisor 2: 2000
    records over 2.40 s, with the one-record-in-six loss that firmware showed
    (398 gaps). Real data on purpose: tests must handle what the firmware
    actually writes.

foxeer-f405-2khz.csv
    What `ferro-configurator convert foxeer-f405-2khz.fwbb --csv` writes for
    it.

foxeer-f405-2khz.ulg.sha256
    The SHA-256 of what `ferro-configurator convert foxeer-f405-2khz.fwbb`
    writes as ULog.

Checked by crates/ferrowasp-protocol/tests/golden_blackbox.rs (decoding,
re-encoding, records), FerroConfigurator's
crates/ferro-configurator-core/tests/golden_blackbox.rs (CSV and ULog bytes),
and tools/tests/test_blackbox_analyzer.py (reading the CSV).

Regenerate the expected outputs only for a deliberate format change, and
review the diff: from tools/ferro-configurator,

    cargo run --release -p ferro-configurator-cli -- convert ../../fixtures/blackbox/foxeer-f405-2khz.fwbb --csv --force

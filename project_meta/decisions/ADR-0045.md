# ADR-0045: Bidirectional DShot Replies Return On Each Lane's Own Pin And Stream

Status: accepted, not yet implemented

Bidirectional DShot gives per-motor eRPM on every frame, for the RPM filter
(ADR-0047) and arming qualification (ADR-0048). The waveform is inverted (idle
high) and the command checksum is inverted. After each frame the ESC answers on
the same wire, about 30 us after the frame ends, with a 21-bit GCR reply at 5/4
of the bit rate.

Each lane receives on its own motor pin through its own DMA stream. When
transmit completes, the stream is disabled, its flags are cleared, its
direction is turned to peripheral-to-memory, and it is re-enabled for the
reply. It turns back before the next frame. No DMA stream is added, and
transmit stays timer compare DMA, not port-wide bitbang.

The Foxeer comes first (W5). Every Foxeer lane samples its pin's GPIO input
register, paced by its own timer channel's DMA request at three samples per
reply bit: 2.25 MHz at DShot600. Input capture cannot serve all four lanes.
Motor 4 is TIM1_CH3N on PB15, and TIM1 channel 3 captures from the CH3 pin,
PA10, which is the USART1 ESC-telemetry wire. Motor 1 shares TIM1, which cannot
both run freely for capture and run at the sampling period. One sampling
decoder serves all four lanes. Betaflight solves the same board with bitbang
DShot on F4. Its receive half, per GPIO port, is the reference.

STM32F405 errata 2.1.10 warns of DMA2 corruption when AHB and APB2 transfers
run concurrently. In the receive window DMA2 reads GPIO, which is on AHB, while
SPI1, ADC1 and USART1 use DMA2 on APB2. Frames start after the IMU transfer
completes (ADR-0046), so that transfer precedes the window, and the ADC trigger
is kept out of it. The ordering is enforced in the schedule and measured on
target before flight. If corruption is measured, SPI1 leaves DMA, as it does in
Betaflight.

A missing, short, or CRC-invalid reply is not a bank fault. It is dropped and
counted per motor (ADR-0047). A reply window still open when the next frame is
due is abandoned and counted. A stream that will not turn back to transmit
faults the bank under ADR-0039, as every transmit fault still does.

Turning a stream around needs direct stream-register access, because the HAL
transfer type fixes direction. That code stays inside the chip backend's
documented unsafe boundary, and the user reviews it. Reply decoding is HAL-free
and host-tested in `ferrowasp-waveform`: GCR, the inverted CRC, the eRPM period,
and extended telemetry frames.

The TBS Lucid H7's lanes are ordinary TIM3 and TIM5 channels. They may capture
on the same stream instead of sampling; that is decided when the Lucid is
brought level.

The inverted waveform is a DShot timing change (W6). It needs logic-analyzer
captures of both directions, the props-off DShot checks, and a flight re-gate.

# ADR-0040: DShot Arming Requires Telemetry-Qualified Idle On Every Motor

Status: accepted; its eRPM source is superseded by ADR-0048 where bidirectional DShot runs

Board-neutral restatement of ADR-0031, recorded on the removed FCU3, and of
ADR-0014's guard, whose PWM arming sequence no longer exists.

After the arm switch and RC qualification, the actuator owner holds stop for a
100 ms dwell, then applies idle (command 65, DShot value 112) under a temporary
permit while the system is still disarmed. Throughout, every 10 ms, it rechecks
the permit, RC armability, the arm switch, low throttle, IMU health, and that no
computer is connected (PR #24): no USB host has configured the board and the
UART configurator link has been silent for 2 s. Any failure selects four stops
before an abort is reported.

After a 250 ms spin-up grace, every physical output must give three consecutive
fresh samples between 3,000 and 10,000 eRPM. Missing, zero, or stale samples
abort at 1.2 s; overspeed aborts after the grace. Every abort selects four
stops. Only full qualification reports preparation complete, and the safety
master repeats its guards before declaring the system armed. The policy is
shared by every board; a board that cannot use it gets its own separately
qualified profile, never one changed field.

ESC telemetry arrives on whichever port is bound to `esc_telemetry`. The ESC
manager has no motor or safety authority. A reply is published only after the
DShot service acknowledges that the frame carrying its request started. A
timeout stops telemetry so late traffic cannot be misattributed. It resumes
only while disarmed, after a quiet window of 500 ms that doubles to at most 4 s
while retries bring nothing back, with every stored sample dropped; this
replaces ADR-0031's latch until reboot (PR #16). The manager waits five seconds
after boot before its first request. Once armed, telemetry loss is
observational and does not by itself disarm.

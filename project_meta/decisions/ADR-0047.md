# ADR-0047: The RPM Filter Follows Betaflight

Status: accepted, not yet implemented

The gyro is filtered by notches that track each motor's speed from
bidirectional eRPM (ADR-0045). The behaviour follows Betaflight's
`flight/rpm_filter.c`, `drivers/dshot.c` and `common/filter.c` at commit
`41a5b43`.

- **Notches.** There is one state-variable notch per motor and harmonic. It is
  applied to all three axes on the unfiltered gyro, ahead of the existing gyro
  low-pass. Its output is the input minus weight times 1/Q times the bandpass.
- **Motor frequency.** The motor frequency is eRPM x 100 / 60 / (poles / 2),
  smoothed by a 150 Hz PT1 at the loop rate. Each notch sits at its harmonic
  of that frequency, clamped between the minimum and 0.48 x the loop rate.
- **Fade.** A notch's weight rises linearly from 0 at the minimum to 1 at the
  minimum plus the fade range, then is scaled by the harmonic's weight. A
  stopped motor reports zero eRPM, and its notches fade out.
- **Coefficient updates.** Coefficients are refreshed round-robin, so that every
  notch is updated within 1 ms. Each update is computed once per motor and
  harmonic and shared by all three axes.
- **Defaults.** 14 poles, 3 harmonics at 100%, a 100 Hz minimum, a 50 Hz fade
  range, Q 5.0 and the 150 Hz frequency low-pass. They are stored
  configuration, a schema change (W6), set through FerroConfigurator.

Invalid replies are dropped, and the last valid eRPM is held without any age
limit in flight. Reply loss neither disarms nor triggers failsafe. This is the
user's explicit decision, and a departure from W2's treatment of stale data as
a defect. Its scope is that stale eRPM moves gyro filtering only, never motor
authority. Arming still fails closed without fresh eRPM (ADR-0048).

Beyond Betaflight, the blackbox records each motor's eRPM, its
consecutive-invalid-reply count and its invalid-reply total. These are
observational only, so held notches can be found in offline analysis.

The filter is HAL-free and host-tested in `ferrowasp-flight`. Its cost per loop
is measured on target. Enabling it changes the gyro filter (W6) and re-gates
flight. The gyro low-pass corner is raised only in steps, after spectral
evidence from logged raw gyro and eRPM shows the notches remove the motor
noise.
